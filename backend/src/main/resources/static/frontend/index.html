Core System Features

Engineered for Rapid Support & Deep Analytics

Built with Spring Boot 3, Spring Security 6, MySQL 8, and Chart.js to ensure high performance, security, and actionable business intelligence.

Automated NLP Sentiment AI

Analyzes incoming complaint text polarity and urgency in real time, automatically escalating critical tickets for rapid triage.

Guaranteed SLA Timelines

Each category features a dynamic Service Level Agreement (SLA) countdown with automatic overdue notifications and escalation flags.

Dual-Role Isolation & Security

Operational Workflow

How a Complaint Moves from Submission to Resolution

A transparent, four-phase automated support journey keeping both customer and support staff aligned.

1
Submit & NLP Triage

Customer submits complaint. System generates unique CMP & TCK numbers while AI analyzes sentiment.

2
Smart Assignment

Complaint is routed to specialized team (Billing, Tech, Logistics) with SLA target clock running.

3
Investigation & Updates

Support staff investigates, updates status to IN_PROGRESS, and notes resolution findings.

4
Resolution & Rating

Ticket is marked RESOLVED. Customer receives notification and rates service via 5-star review.
